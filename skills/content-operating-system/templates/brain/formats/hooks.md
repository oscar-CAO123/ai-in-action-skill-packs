# Hook structures

Never free-write a hook. Pick a structure by id, fill its slots with specifics, and record the id
beside the hook. That is the citation law, and it is the only thing that makes learning possible: a
hook with no id cannot be compared to anything.

Every structure below needs at least one real example before it is used in production. A structure
with no example is a guess with a name on it.

| Id | Shape | Our example |
|---|---|---|
| `avatar-cost` | [Role] are losing [specific measure] to [specific cause] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `objection-honest` | "[Objection in their words]" is fair. Here is what it misses | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `wrong-first-move` | Most [role] start with [common first attempt]. That is why [failure] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `changed-mind` | I was wrong about [thing] for [duration]. What changed it was [event] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `number-surprise` | We looked at [N] [things]. [Counterintuitive finding] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `demonstration` | This is [process] as it actually happens, including [ugly part] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `insider` | Here is what happens on our side when you ask for [common request] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `refusal` | We turn down [type of work]. The reason is [specific] | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `overheard` | A customer said "[exact quote]". Here is what that actually means | {{EXAMPLE}} |
| `two-paths` | You can [A] or [B]. Most pick A and [consequence] | {{EXAMPLE}} |

## Rules for filling one

- Every slot gets a specific. An abstract slot fill produces an abstract hook regardless of the
  structure.
- The foreshadow is not optional. "Here is what it misses" is a foreshadow. "Watch this" is not.
- Say it out loud. A hook that does not survive being spoken will not survive being spoken on camera.
- The gap sits at the audience's pain, never at our product.
