# Buyer archetypes

> **This ships empty on purpose.** The original held one company's own material, which would make
> your output look like theirs. The structure below is the part that transfers. Fill it in once and
> every format in this repo starts reading from it.
>
> Fastest way to fill it: run `funnel-builder` or `member-business-interview` in the same workspace
> first. Their corpus pass produces most of what goes here, in your buyers' own words.

Who you are writing to. One entry per archetype, three or four total.

```
name: <what you would call them in the office>
situation: <what is happening in their business right now>
believes: <what they think the problem is>
actually true: <what the problem is>
words they use: <verbatim, from recordings>
words they never use: <usually your category jargon>
what they need to see: <the proof that moves them>
right for the offer: yes | no
```

The funnel-builder skill's corpus pass produces this. Do not write it from memory.
