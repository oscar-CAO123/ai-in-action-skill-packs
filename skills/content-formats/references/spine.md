# The spine, what good looks like

> **This ships empty on purpose.** The original held one company's own material, which would make
> your output look like theirs. The structure below is the part that transfers. Fill it in once and
> every format in this repo starts reading from it.
>
> Fastest way to fill it: run `funnel-builder` or `member-business-interview` in the same workspace
> first. Their corpus pass produces most of what goes here, in your buyers' own words.

Three to five canonical entries. Read first, every time, before writing a word.

## Entry template

**The piece.** Paste it verbatim. Copy, script or caption.
**Where it ran and what it did.** Placement, spend if paid, and the one number that made you keep it.
**The structure underneath.** Beat by beat, in your words: what each line is doing.
**The mechanism.** The single reason it worked. Not "it was punchy". Something like "it named the
exact moment the reader last felt the problem".
**What to copy and what not to.** The shape transfers. The specifics usually do not.
