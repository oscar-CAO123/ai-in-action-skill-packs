# Guru clip concepts (F1, talkshow-vsl)

Twelve written concepts, one per **pain family**, selected off the unified pain wiki by how many
separate companies carry the pain. Each is a 20 to 40s guru clip: the distinguished older
Australian guest on the 1970s set, delivering one weighty truth to an unseen host, naming the
offer on the last line.

**How to use this file.** Pick a concept, copy its script into `vo/lines.txt` one line per line
(each line is a clip unit), then run the pipeline in `SKILL.md` from Phase 3. The concept fields
are already resolved, so Phase 0 only has to confirm mode, aspect and the hero still.

## Selection rule: breadth, not persona

**These twelve are deliberately not avatar-specific.** No trade, no vertical, no named persona,
no industry callout appears in any script. The selection rule is company spread in
`context/research-corpus/INDEX.md`: every pain below is carried by at least 31 separate companies, so
each script is written to the pain a business owner has regardless of what the business sells.

The wiki's 33 themes collapse to these twelve families once the duplicates the queue already
resolved are merged (`growth-capped` with `scaling-without-headcount`, `fragile-diy-systems`
with `failed-diy-ai`, `ai-know-how-gap` with `option-overwhelm` and `no-time-to-implement`).

**Still the right selection rule after the restructure.** The wiki now leads with 21
industry playbooks, but this set is deliberately general-pain, so it draws from `pains/<slug>.md`
rather than `industries/<slug>.md` and that is correct. Two things to carry across: rank additions
off the weighted table in `context/research-corpus/MARKET.md` rather than off record counts, and note that
four of these twelve families (the DIY-AI ones, the know-how ones) are scored as objections in the
report, not pains, so their evidence sits in `context/research-corpus/objections/` under Prior Bad
Experience and Proof And Scepticism.

**Three angles from the earlier persona-keyed bank were dropped on this rule** and are available
as Tier 2 rotation whenever the vertical work starts: A6 the owner personally building AI (16
companies), A7 quoting and estimating (21), A11 risk and governance (8 for `ai-governance-gap`,
23 for `data-security-fear`). Each is real and each is narrow, so none of them earns one of
twelve slots aimed at the whole market. The persona-keyed version of this file is preserved at
`_versions/IDEAS-avatar-keyed-20260801.md`.

**Grounding.** Every pain traces to a theme page in `context/research-corpus/pains/`, cited in the
concept's Wiki line. Every build named in a teach line traces to a published Hub build with its
recorded outcome, cited in the Teach line. **No verbatim, company name or census count appears in
any script**: the wiki records are client PII, so they select the concept and never enter the
copy. Where a figure belongs to one deployment, the script says one business.

**Prevalence carries the proof, not an anecdote.** The guest speaks from the pattern rather than
from a case. He has watched four decades of businesses and describes the room he keeps walking
into, which is why no script needs a company, a trade or a number he could not have. The
specificity lands on the mechanism and on the recorded outcome instead.

**Register.** Cinematic Register 1 shifted older and drier. He has watched businesses rise and
fall and is unimpressed by all of it. He observes, he never sells.

**Line 1 is a fill, never a free-write.** Eleven of the twelve hooks below
are taken from `../../references/scripts/core-pain-vsl-scripts.md`, the eighteen core-pain VSL
scripts mined from real client and lead calls, and each concept cites the script number it came
from. `../../references/hooks/HOOKS.md` supplies the mechanics floor those hooks are graded
against: ten to fifteen words a sentence, a second loop before the fifteen-second mark, and
discomfort inside the first three seconds.

**The arc, compressed from the canonical P/S/VSL spine.** That corpus runs
one structure for all eighteen scripts and this format runs the same one inside 110 words. Line 1
opens the gap. Lines 2 to 4 are the machine walkthrough, "here is your business, the work comes in
here, this is where it breaks", carrying the specific pain detail that does the agitating. Lines 5
and 6 educate across the gap with one real build and its recorded outcome. Lines 7 and 8 resolve
it, and house is named nowhere before line 8.

**One conflict with that corpus.** Its canonical pivot, "But the fix isn't X, it's Y", and several
of its hooks run the contrast negation, which section 1 banned on after those scripts
were written on . Every hook below is rebuilt into the positive form. Read the source
for structure rather than for wording.

**The word budget is hard and it is the binding constraint.** F1 runs 55 to 110 spoken words and
nothing else (`content-formats/SKILL.md` 7c). Every script below is counted and lands between 100 and
110, which is the top of the band, roughly 38 seconds at this register. Adding a line means
cutting one.

**Every script teaches, with one build rather than three.** The education law
(`content-formats/SKILL.md` section 1) binds in every format, and 7c now writes the F1 shape down:
one build, plus its recorded outcome. The trio belongs to F5, where three slides carry it. In
thirty-eight seconds of one man talking, three builds eat the pain arc and land nothing.

# Full VSL mode: the three-system fork

**This is a fork of one script, not a composition.** The source is
`../../references/scripts/core-pain-vsl-scripts.md` #8, the owner-bottleneck P/S/VSL, and its
structure and arc are copied whole. One block changes: where the source turns to the hire, the
fork turns to three AI systems and teaches them.

**The arc, as forked.**

1. **The hook**, verbatim from the source. Either the dream outcome as a question, or the pain
   agitated. #8 uses the dream outcome.
2. **The turn.** "Well, you can. You just need these three AI systems." One line, and it goes
   straight on. The objection pre-empt that sat here ("and no, it's not hiring a
   second-in-command") was cut .
3. **Straight into the systems.** No machine walkthrough, no "here's your business, the work
   comes in here". That beat is the source's own and it is cut: the purpose of this script is to
   educate, so the runtime goes to the builds.
4. **Three AI systems, each taught the same way: the tools, what it does, the outcome.** Every
   one is a real published Hub build, and the build's own `replicate_steps` is where its tools
   come from rather than the abstracted `stack` column.
   **The tools beat is spoken in the owner's language, not the builder's.** The viewer does not
   know what LangGraph or a classifier model is, and naming them costs the line. Answer the
   question the owner is actually asking, which is what it plugs into and what it touches: the
   software you already pay for, your inbox, your team chat. The technical stack stays in the
   concept metadata as the evidence trail and never reaches the script.
5. **The bridge.** One sentence between the third outcome and the CTA: all three are already
   running somewhere, and the gap widens every month it is left. Honest scarcity, no overclaim,
   because trust is the thing this brand cannot spend.
6. **The quiz close**, in place of "there's only one place in Australia you can hire them", and
   carried in on "so" rather than starting cold.

Forty five to ninety seconds, roughly 120 to 240 spoken words. **The role is never named in this
mode**, so the quiz result is what introduces the category.

**V01 is the canonical style, locked by you .** Every full VSL gets written against
it. The style is written down in `talkshow-vsl/SKILL.md` Phase 2 and in `content-formats/SKILL.md`
7c; this file holds the reference implementation. The remaining eleven pains are unwritten.

## V01 · The weekend off (G02 pain, VSL mode, forked from core-pain-vsl-scripts #8)

- **Pain** `owner-bottleneck`, 116 records / 98 companies, the second-largest theme in the wiki ·
  **Angle** A2 the owner bottleneck · **Offer** none named · **Posture** general pain
- **Hook line (persistent, top of frame)** EVERY DECISION STILL WAITING ON YOU
- **Fork source** `references/scripts/core-pain-vsl-scripts.md` #8. The hook is that script's own
  words. Its machine walkthrough is cut, and its hire block is replaced by the three AI systems.
- **Builds, all three published in the Hub `builds` table** (project `your-project-ref`),
  chosen for the size of the recorded time saving and because each one takes the owner out of a
  different moment: the doing, the answering, the reporting. **All three have to be industry
  agnostic**, because this asset is aimed at the whole market rather than a vertical. The three in
  `noir-pain-bottleneck` were dropped (no tools recorded, and an executive assistant is the thing
  this owner has already tried), and so were the router and the morning brief (four roles saved
  and twenty changes a day are real, but neither is a saving an owner feels personally). Slot two
  went through two more on : the week-long verification build sat too close to slot
  three, and the warranty dispatcher was field-service specific, so its twenty one days to four
  bought nothing for an owner with no technicians to dispatch.
  1. *Enterprise multi-agent workflow automation with security controls*, enterprise services.
     Tools from its own `replicate_steps`: LangGraph for state and routing, LangChain for the
     model calls, a hosted model as the reasoning engine, every downstream system wrapped as a
     Python tool, Langfuse for the audit log. Outcome: manual workflow overhead down about sixty
     percent, still in production.
  2. *Email-automation product that saves each client 1-2 hours a day.* OAuth into Gmail or
     Microsoft, deterministic rules first so an obvious notification never costs a model call, a
     small vision model to catch the edge cases those rules would misread, about fifty
     specialised classifiers rather than one large model, auto-draft or auto-send by confidence
     level, and a follow-up monitor that surfaces anything unanswered after three days with the
     reminder already drafted. Outcome: 17 paying business clients, saving one to two hours a
     day each.
  3. *Adaptive AI platform that turns a quarterly board-pack process into one click*, research and
     development. Source data modelled into canonical tables, embeddings for retrieval, outputs
     constrained to be deterministic, and provenance on every figure so it traces to source.
