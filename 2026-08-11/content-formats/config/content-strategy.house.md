# Content strategy

> **This ships empty on purpose.** The original held one company's own material, which would make
> your output look like theirs. The structure below is the part that transfers. Fill it in once and
> every format in this repo starts reading from it.
>
> Fastest way to fill it: run `funnel-builder` or `member-business-interview` in the same workspace
> first. Their corpus pass produces most of what goes here, in your buyers' own words.

The operating contract, so ideation does not renegotiate strategy every week.

```
the barbell: <what you make a lot of, cheaply, and what you make rarely and expensively>
format families: <which formats, and roughly what share of output>
cadence: <per channel, per week>
the one job: <what all of it is for: attention, teaching, or a sale>
what you will not make: <the formats you have decided against, and why>
review: <the number you check, and when>
```
