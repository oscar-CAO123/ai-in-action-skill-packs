# The hook register

A hook line is the two-line headline plated over the old card. Generic hook banks were rejected
twice on real people as flat. Draft in the speaker's own register, show the lines, take the user's
edits verbatim, including their punctuation and any glyph.

Shapes that worked, with placeholder examples:

| Shape | Example |
|---|---|
| Direct address, a stop | "Business owners: stop trying to be the expert" |
| Don't X before Y | "Don't hire a consultant before watching this" |
| The real reason | "The real reason this isn't working in your business" |
| Why you're actually | "Why you're actually struggling to scale" |
| No clue? Watch this | "No clue how to start? Watch this" |
| One X can Y | "One right hire can 10x your business" |
| A comparison with a glyph | "More software ≠ better systems" |
| A number with a horizon | "Half of all businesses gone in 5 years" |

Two lines, the break where the breath is. The plate fits the text down from 58 px until both lines
fit inside the card's box with a 30 px side margin. A line the plate cannot fit at 30 px is too long.
