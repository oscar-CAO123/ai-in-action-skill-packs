## The Computer Use folder

One parent, four skills, and the order inside it is the order to try them in: an official API first,
then [`build-headless-cli`](../skills/computer-use/build-headless-cli), then
[`build-deterministic-macro`](../skills/computer-use/build-deterministic-macro), then
[`codex-computer-use`](../skills/computer-use/codex-computer-use) when the layout moves and the job
needs eyes. [`linkedin-outreach`](../skills/computer-use/linkedin-outreach) is the worked example
that puts all of it in one job with an approval gate on every send.

## The content pipeline, codified

The four phases on the deck are one skill: [`content-operating-system`](../skills/content-operating-system).
A workspace sweep, a 60 to 90 minute interview, then a working brain, engine and outputs tree built
out of your own customer evidence.

- **Data.** Ingestion pulls calls, tickets, forums and reviews nightly into a redacted store.
- **Idea.** Ideation clusters and ranks that weekly and hands back a queue with the quotes attached.
- **Production.** Stays attached to a person, behind a spend guard that refuses without an approved
  batch id. The formats themselves are [`content-formats`](../skills/content-formats) and
  [`seedance-prompt`](../skills/seedance-prompt).
- **Publish.** What performs goes back into the data layer.

Two scheduled tasks and nothing else.
