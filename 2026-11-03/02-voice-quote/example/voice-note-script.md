# The example voice note

Read this aloud into the Record button (about 40 seconds), or upload a recording of it. The same words
are in `transcript.txt`, which is what **Use the example** puts in the box. The job, the name and the
address are made up.

> Okay, quote for Jo Sample at 12 Sample Street. Jo wants a new Colorbond fence across the back and
> down one side, Woodland Grey, one point eight high. The back is eighteen metres and the side is
> fourteen, so thirty two metres in total. One pedestrian gate on the side. The old timber fence comes
> out and gets taken away. A concrete path runs along part of the side, so a few posts will need core
> drilling. Jo also asked about lattice on top of the back run for privacy. Sometime in the next six
> weeks would suit.

## What should come back, with the example price list

- 32 m of Woodland Grey Colorbond at 1.8 m, 1 gate, the old fence taken away, wanted in the next six weeks.
- $11,833.14 including GST ($8,964.50 cost, plus the 20% markup, plus 10% GST).
- Core drilling and the lattice are on no price list line, so they come back as **Needs a price** at
  $0. Price them yourself before you send.
- The posts in the path, the lattice size and the neighbour's notice land in **Confirm before it goes**.

A live reading by your LLM can word things a little differently from the saved one. The facts and the
total should match.
