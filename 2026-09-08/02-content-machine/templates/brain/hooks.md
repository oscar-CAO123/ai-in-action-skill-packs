# Hooks

Structures with ids and slots. Never a filled line: a filled line gets reused verbatim within a
fortnight and every piece starts to sound the same.

---

## `hook:S1` <the structure's name>

**Shape.** <the pattern, with its slots marked, e.g. "<figure> <unit> <who> <verb> <object>">

**Works when.** <the condition that makes this shape land>

**Fails when.** <where it reads as a gimmick>

**Cited from.** <the piece this was read off, so the structure has a source>
