# Copy laws

Every law here should be checkable by a script. `engine/gates/copy.py` reads this file, and the
copy gate fails a batch rather than warning about it.

## Banned words and phrases

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| <word> | <what to say> |

## The claim rule

<What may be asserted plainly, and what needs a named proof beside it. Name the proof file.>

## Refused structures

<Sentence shapes and rhetorical moves this business does not use. State the shape, not an example
you will end up reusing.>

## Formatting

<Punctuation, capitalisation, numerals, and anything that appears in every piece.>

## Tone

<Written as things to do. Adjectives are not checkable, and neither is "sound human".>
