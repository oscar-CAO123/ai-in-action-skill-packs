# The AI tells ban list

> **This ships empty on purpose.** The original held one company's own material, which would make
> your output look like theirs. The structure below is the part that transfers. Fill it in once and
> every format in this repo starts reading from it.
>
> Fastest way to fill it: run `funnel-builder` or `member-business-interview` in the same workspace
> first. Their corpus pass produces most of what goes here, in your buyers' own words.

Words and constructions that make copy read as machine-written. Run a scan over anything before it
goes out.

**Vocabulary:** leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, harness, game-changing, revolutionary,
cutting-edge, transformative, robust, synergy, ecosystem, supercharge, next-generation, paradigm
shift, elevate, delve, testament, tapestry, landscape, realm, navigate, foster, embark.

**Phrases:** "in the age of AI", "with the rise of", "the future of", "this changes everything",
"level up", "master the art of", "it is worth noting", "in today's fast-paced".

**Constructions:**
- **The negation swap.** "It is not X, it is Y." Say the positive thing directly.
- **The rule of three, every time.** Real writing varies.
- **Em dashes.** Use a comma, a period, a colon or parentheses.
- **Hedging stacks.** "It might perhaps be somewhat".
- **Restating the question before answering it.**

Add your own as you catch them. This list is only useful if it grows from real drafts.
