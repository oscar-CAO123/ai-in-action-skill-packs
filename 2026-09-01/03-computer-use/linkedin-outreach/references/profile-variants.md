# Profile variants

Keep your own profile-schema map alongside this file and re-capture it whenever the platform drifts.

Captured variants include owner Connect inside More, Follow with no Connect, and bare More with no owner-named buttons. The reliable owner boundary is the top-card section containing the profile heading. More-menu Connect may be plain text without an accessible label. Pending may disappear after a confirmed invitation, so the local contacted ledger is the duplicate guard. Original-case obfuscated URLs must be preserved for navigation.
